Privacy policy

Jonny is a private assistant used only by the family who run it. Only family members sign in with their Google accounts, and only by their own choice.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads email to spot what matters, such as school letters and bookings, and sends an email only after a family member has approved that exact message.
  • Google Calendar: reads events, and adds or changes them on the family's shared calendar.
  • Google Drive: reads files when a family member needs them.
  • Google Cloud Pub/Sub: receives a notice that new email has arrived.

How the data is handled

  • Sign-in tokens and notes are stored on the family's own computer and in their private code repository.
  • Content is sent to the AI service that powers Jonny, Anthropic's Claude, so it can be read and acted on.
  • It is used only to help the family. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else, except where the family asks Jonny to send something.

Removing access

Anyone who has signed in can remove access at any time from their Google account's permissions page.
